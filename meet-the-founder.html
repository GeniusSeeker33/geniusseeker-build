Meet the Founder

Desiree Thayer, Founder of GeniusSeeker.com.

A talent acquisition professional with over 25 years of experience, Desiree founded GeniusSeeker to help people recognize their value, connect their gifts to meaningful work, and build organizations rooted in truth and accountability.

She believes hiring is broken not because people lack talent, but because too many systems were never designed to see that talent clearly — and that responsible technology, used in service of people rather than in place of them, can help fix that.

In addition to her work in technology, Desiree is a musician and lifelong horsewoman who leads the LeopardFriesian - Echoes of the Bloodline initiative focused on equine breeding and healing.

What we’re building

A human-centered talent and organizational transformation platform — one that helps people discover what they do best and helps organizations build environments where that talent can thrive.

That means human insight and responsible technology working together — with people, not profiles or token balances, always at the center.
