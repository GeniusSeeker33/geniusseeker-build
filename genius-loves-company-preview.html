How it started

Genius has always loved company.

Genius Loves Company grew out of a collaboration between Desiree Thayer and Wesley Allen of Eastwood Records, rooted in a shared love of music.

At EarthTone Analog, Desiree welcomed wonderfully original artists into what she affectionately called “the land of misfit toys.” Recording, singing harmonies with John Clay and the Boxwine Prophets, and collaborating on the 2017 Louisville Muse Cruise became part of that adventure.

Today, that same spirit continues: bring people together, give talent room to grow, and see what we can create.

“I wanted to host all of these incredible musicians and experience music in first person.”

— Desiree Thayer, founder
