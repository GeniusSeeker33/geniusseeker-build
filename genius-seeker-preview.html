Built from real experience

People first.
Practice with a purpose.

Founded by Desiree Thayer, GeniusSeeker draws on more than 25 years of recruiting experience and a belief that people need opportunities to develop as well as opportunities to work.

Orion Wholesale is the first workplace pilot for the training and coaching approach. The focus is practical: role-play, useful feedback, and skills people can apply in their work.

The broader platform is still being built. Contact Desiree to discuss hiring support, explore a pilot, or learn what is available for your team.
